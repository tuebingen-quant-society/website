Where would you look for someone who can test a trading signal, work out why
a model fails on new data, or implement an inference method? Tübingen has
several routes into that work. Its students can study statistical learning,
numerical methods and financial econometrics, and its graduates have taken
those skills into quantitative research, systematic investing and power trading.

## What students can study

The course catalogue gives students and recruiters a concrete way to judge
the training. These are examples from Tübingen's computer science and
economics departments.

Hennig's research group studies **probabilistic numerics**. It asks how a
numerical algorithm can reason about uncertainty in its own approximations,
including when it solves optimization problems or differential equations.
That focus on the reliability of a computation is relevant wherever a model's
output informs a decision.
[Chair for the Methods of Machine Learning](https://uni-tuebingen.de/en/fakultaeten/mathematisch-naturwissenschaftliche-fakultaet/fachbereiche/informatik/lehrstuehle/methods-of-machine-learning/start/)

Tübingen also offers master's degrees in Economics and Finance and in
Quantitative Data Science Methods. The latter connects econometrics,
psychometrics and machine learning through training in statistics, study
design and programming. Students can follow an economics route into
financial modelling or build a broader foundation in statistical inference.
[M.Sc. Economics and Finance](https://uni-tuebingen.de/fakultaeten/wirtschafts-und-sozialwissenschaftliche-fakultaet/faecher/fachbereich-wirtschaftswissenschaft/wirtschaftswissenschaft/studium/studiengaenge/master/msc-economics-and-finance/) ·
[M.Sc. Quantitative Data Science Methods](https://uni-tuebingen.de/en/study/finding-a-course/degree-programs-available/detail/course/quantitative-data-science-methods-psychometrics-econometrics-and-machine-learning-master/)

## Machine learning and the natural sciences

Tübingen's scientific tradition stretches from Johannes Kepler, who studied
there, to Friedrich Miescher's isolation of nuclein in Tübingen in 1869.
Today's physics department researches quantum science and astrophysics. Its
master's programme builds on theoretical training that includes statistical
physics, thermodynamics and quantum mechanics. Working with simulations and
measurements teaches students to connect a mathematical model to data.
[University history](https://uni-tuebingen.de/en/university/profile/history-of-the-university/) ·
[Physics research](https://uni-tuebingen.de/en/faculties/faculty-of-science/departments/physics/research/) ·
[M.Sc. Physics](https://uni-tuebingen.de/en/faculties/faculty-of-science/departments/physics/studies/study-programs/msc-physics/)

In March 2026, Germany's Excellence Commission renewed Tübingen's status
as a University of Excellence, with another seven years of funding from
2027. Its **Machine Learning: New Perspectives for Science** Cluster of
Excellence was funded again for 2026–2032. The cluster brings machine
learning to scientific problems and lists the Max Planck Institute for
Intelligent Systems and the ELLIS Institute Tübingen among its partners.
[University of Excellence decision](https://www.wissenschaftsrat.de/SharedDocs/Pressemitteilungen/EN/PM_0826_ExStra) ·
[Machine Learning Cluster of Excellence](https://www.exzellenzstrategie.de/en/clusters-of-excellence/machine-learning-for-science/)

The cluster reported **53 papers accepted at NeurIPS 2026** from its
researchers and their teams. Its list includes work on benchmarking tabular
machine learning and on prediction systems whose outputs affect what they
later observe. Both are relevant questions in finance, where much of the data
is tabular and decisions can change the environment a model operates in.
[Cluster announcement and paper list](https://uni-tuebingen.de/forschung/forschungsschwerpunkte/exzellenzcluster-maschinelles-lernen/home/home-newsfullview/article/53-paper-bei-neurips-2026-akzeptiert/)

## Where Tübingen graduates work

The [Tübingen alumni profiles](/en#alumni) show what those disciplines look
like in industry. Mario Schröck moved from theoretical physics into
quantitative research at Squarepoint. Felix Leibfried went from doctoral
work in AI to systematic trading research at Eisler Capital, quantitative
development at iSAM Funds and AI engineering at Millennium. Patrick Voßkamp
wrote an award-winning thesis on factor investing and went on to co-lead
systematic equity at Allianz Global Investors. Max Amir Dieringer took an
economics background into power trading and co-founded FlexPower, which
Citadel acquired.

At Tübingen Quant Society, we bring students across those disciplines
together. Our [derivatives workshop](/en/article/options-futures-perpetuals)
uses pricing models and simulations to connect mathematical finance with
market conventions. Students can start there or explore the courses above.
Employers who want to meet students working on these problems can
[get in touch with the society](/en#partner).
